Privacy Policy

Application: finance-sync
Operator: Julius Wiener (gyptii@gmail.com)
Last updated: February 2026

1. Purpose

finance-sync is a personal tool used solely by its operator to access and store their own bank account data. It is not a public service and does not process data of third parties.

2. Data accessed

The application accesses account information (balances and transactions) from the operator's own bank accounts via the Enable Banking API. No data is shared with third parties.

3. Data storage

All data is stored locally on the operator's private server. No cloud storage or external databases are used.
